How Pure Sphere Works

Three effortless steps to a perfectly clean helmet every single time

01

Place Your Helmet

Open the Pure Sphere pod and place your helmet inside the custom-fit chamber. The smart sensor automatically detects your helmet size and adjusts the cleaning protocol.

02

Select & Start

Choose your cleaning mode via the touchscreen — Quick Clean (5 min), Deep Clean (15 min), or Sanitize (8 min). Close the lid and the intelligent system begins automatically.

03

Collect & Ride

Your helmet emerges spotless, odour-free, and 99.9% bacteria-free. The pod alerts you when the cycle is done. Open, collect, and ride with confidence.
